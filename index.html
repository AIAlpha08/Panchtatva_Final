Common questions

Who is Panchtatva for?

Businesses and institutions: offices, contract sites, corporate lunches and meetings. For a single meal, please visit Shudh Annapurna or order on Swiggy or Zomato.

Can I order for every day?

Contract catering is one of our services. Choose how often you need food in the form and we will discuss it with you.

Do you cover my location?

Enter your location in the form and our team will confirm. Our business address is in Jhandewalan Extension, New Delhi.

How soon will you reply?

Our team calls you on the number you give. For urgent requirements, call 7703-874377 directly.
